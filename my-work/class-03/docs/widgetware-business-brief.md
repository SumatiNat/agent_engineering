# WidgetWare Business Brief

## Overview

WidgetWare sells enterprise software and advisory services to manufacturing and industrial-automation companies to modernize plant operations and adopt AI-enabled automation.

## Offerings

1. **Plant Operations Platform**: Consolidates operational data across plants, enabling real-time monitoring and modernization initiatives.
2. **Industrial AI Accelerator**: Helps industrial organizations identify, evaluate, and implement governed AI use cases.

## Target Buyers

- **Plant Operations & Manufacturing**: VP of Manufacturing, Plant Operations Director, Chief Digital Officer.
- **Technology & AI Leadership**: Chief Technology Officer, VP of Digital Transformation, Head of Industrial AI.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

- **Target Enterprise Scale**: 5,000+ employees.
- **Preferred Sectors**: Manufacturing, industrial automation, automotive manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, industrial equipment.
- **Excluded Sectors**: Consumer retail, restaurants, personal services.
- **Key Buying Signals**: Plant modernization initiatives, digital transformation announcements, new AI executive hires, or manufacturing data projects.

## SDR Deliverables & Boundaries

- The SDR context package prepares structured facts, evidence provenance, and ICP alignment for evaluation.
- All factual claims must be supported by supplied evidence or explicitly classified as inferences.
- **Human Approval Boundary**: External outreach (email/social messaging), CRM modifications, pricing quotes, and contractual statements strictly require explicit human approval and are prohibited for automated execution.
